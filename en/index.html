Is the reception open at night?

Yes. The reception is open 24 hours a day, every day, so late arrivals are welcome.

When is breakfast served?

Every day from 07:00 to 10:00. The hotel confirms whether breakfast is included in your rate when it replies to your enquiry.

How do I find the hotel?

Enter the Plus Code Q8VW+WJ Andijan in Google Maps, or tap “Get directions” in the Location section. Both open the exact pin.

Does this website reserve my room?

No. The form sends an enquiry. The hotel replies to confirm availability and the final price; only then is the room held for you.

Is there parking, and can I pay by card?

These details are being confirmed with the hotel. Mention them in your enquiry and the hotel will answer.
